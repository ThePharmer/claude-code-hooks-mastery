# Crypto News Research

Research and summarize the latest cryptocurrency news, market developments, and emerging trends.

## Instructions

- **IMPORTANT: Focus on recent news (last 24-48 hours)**
- **IMPORTANT: Prioritize high-impact developments**
- **IMPORTANT: Include both macro trends and specific cryptocurrencies**
- **IMPORTANT: Provide source context and credibility assessment**

## Research Focus Areas

1. **Market Movements**
   - Major price changes (>10% moves)
   - Market cap shifts
   - Trading volume anomalies

2. **Regulatory Developments**
   - New regulations or enforcement actions
   - Government statements on crypto
   - Institutional adoption news

3. **Technology Updates**
   - Major protocol upgrades
   - New blockchain launches
   - DeFi innovations

4. **Security Events**
   - Hacks or exploits
   - Security audits
   - Vulnerability disclosures

5. **Institutional Activity**
   - Corporate treasury moves
   - ETF developments
   - Major investments or partnerships

## Output Format

Provide:
- **Top Headlines**: 5-7 most significant stories
- **Market Impact**: How news affects markets
- **Key Takeaways**: Actionable insights
- **Watch List**: Cryptocurrencies or events to monitor
- **Risk Factors**: Potential concerns or red flags

## Default Behavior

If no specific focus provided, research top cryptocurrency news across all categories from the last 24 hours.
